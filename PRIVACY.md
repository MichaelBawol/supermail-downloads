# Supermail Privacy Policy

**Effective: 3 September 2026**

Supermail is a desktop email client. Its developer does not operate a mail proxy, advertising service or analytics service, and does not receive your mailbox credentials, messages, contacts or app-usage history.

## Mail accounts and local data

Supermail connects from your Mac directly to the mail providers you configure. Mail account settings, passwords, OAuth tokens, drafts, cached mail, local contacts, reminders, assistant settings and assistant history are stored on your Mac. Supermail encrypts its structured local stores using macOS secure storage. Password-protected backup files are encrypted separately with the password you choose.

Your mail provider necessarily receives your account and mail requests in order to provide email service. Its privacy policy and retention practices apply. Exported attachments, diagnostics and backups go only to a location you choose.

Supermail does not contain advertising or third-party analytics and does not track you across apps or websites. The direct-download edition contacts the public Supermail GitHub release feed for update checks. The Mac App Store edition receives updates from Apple.

## Kitty assistant

Kitty is optional. You choose each AI connection, which may be Codex with ChatGPT sign-in, an API provider, a local model server or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Before Kitty uses a connection, Supermail shows the destination and asks you to allow either general chat or inbox assistance.

General chat can share your questions and recent Kitty replies with that connection. Inbox assistance can also share relevant account email addresses, message senders and recipients, subjects, message text, and the instructions and results of routines you enable. Kitty fetches mail when needed for your request or routine; connecting it does not upload your inbox. Attachments and mailbox passwords are excluded. Mail actions and reply drafts require your review.

AI providers may retain or use information according to their own terms, account settings and privacy policies. A local endpoint remains on your Mac only if that server is configured not to forward requests. Supermail does not receive copies of AI requests or replies.

You may narrow mail access per request. Revoking a connection's permission stops active Kitty work and pauses its routines, but cannot recall information already sent to the chosen provider. Changing its provider address or executable requires fresh permission.

## System integrations

Contacts and Reminders access starts only when you use the related feature and macOS grants permission. Recipient suggestions imported from Contacts are stored locally. Supermail may ask macOS to create a reminder you review. You can revoke these permissions in macOS System Settings.

## Your choices and deletion

You can remove mail or AI connections, clear Kitty history, clear cached mail, and delete local data by removing Supermail's app data or uninstalling the app. Remove information retained by a mail or AI provider through that provider's account controls.

Questions and privacy reports can be submitted through the [public Supermail support tracker](https://github.com/MichaelBawol/supermail-downloads/issues). Do not include passwords, API keys, OAuth tokens or private email content in a public report.

This policy will be updated before a release if Supermail's data practices materially change.
